Health Hope at UCLA

We are two UCLA students building something simple: free health workshops, real mentorship, and screenings for communities that have been overlooked. No corporate backing. Just us showing up.

Join Our Team → Our Story

We kept seeing the same thing — people in our communities falling through the cracks. Not because help didn't exist, but because nobody brought it to them.

When we got to UCLA, we realized we had something valuable: knowledge, connections, and the energy to actually do something. So we started Health Hope. Because we think a 15-minute conversation about diabetes prevention can genuinely change someone's life.

We are still early. Still growing. But every flyer we hand out and every student we mentor gets us a little closer to the LA we want to see.

What We Do

Programs that go to the people.

We do not hold events and hope the right people find us. We partner with organizations, go to underserved neighborhoods, and bring health education directly to the people who need it most.
🏥

Mobile Screenings

Free blood pressure, glucose, BMI, and vision assessments in underserved neighborhoods.

🩺

Suturing and Vitals Workshops

Hands-on clinical skills training with real techniques used in healthcare settings.

🎓

Career Mentorship

One-on-one guidance for students exploring medicine, nursing, and public health careers.

🧠

Mental Health

Open forums and workshops to destigmatize mental health in underserved communities.

🌍

Community Outreach

Going to shelters, schools, and community centers to distribute health materials.

📋

Health Literacy

Teaching disease prevention, nutrition basics, and healthcare system navigation.

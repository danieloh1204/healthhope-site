Why We Started

We kept seeing the same problem — and decided to do something about it.

Growing up, we watched people in our communities struggle to access basic health information. Not because it did not exist, but because nobody brought it to them. Doctor visits were expensive. Health literacy was low. Preventable diseases went undetected.

When we got to UCLA, we realized we had the knowledge, the network, and the energy to change that. So we started Health Hope — not as a resume line, but because we genuinely believe that a 15-minute conversation about diabetes prevention or a free blood pressure check can change the trajectory of someone's life.

We are still early. We are still growing. But every flyer we hand out, every student we mentor, and every community we visit gets us closer to a Los Angeles where healthcare access is not a privilege.

What We Do

Programs That Meet People Where They Are

We do not wait for communities to come to us. We go to them.

🏥

Mobile Health Screenings

Free blood pressure, glucose, BMI, and vision assessments brought directly to underserved neighborhoods.

🩺

Suturing and Vital Signs Workshops

Hands-on clinical skills training where students learn real techniques used in healthcare settings.

🎓

Healthcare Career Mentorship

One-on-one mentoring for high school and college students exploring careers in medicine and public health.

🧠

Mental Health Initiatives

Open forums and workshops to destigmatize mental health and connect people with support resources.

🌍

Community Outreach

Partnering with local organizations to distribute health education materials and connect with underserved populations.

📋

Health Literacy Education

Teaching communities about disease prevention, nutrition, and how to navigate the healthcare system.
